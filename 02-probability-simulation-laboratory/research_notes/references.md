# References

Textbooks and papers consulted for the mathematics (all are standard published works; page/theorem numbers are deliberately not quoted).

**Probability and statistics**
* S. Ross, *A First Course in Probability* (Pearson).
* G. Grimmett and D. Stirzaker, *Probability and Random Processes* (Oxford University Press).
* W. Feller, *An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications*, Vol. 1 (Wiley).
* R. Durrett, *Probability: Theory and Examples* (Cambridge University Press).
* J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains* (Cambridge University Press).
* P. Billingsley, *Convergence of Probability Measures* (Wiley).

* S. Shreve, *Stochastic Calculus for Finance II: Continuous-Time Models* (Springer).

* J. Hull, *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* (Pearson).
* F. Black and M. Scholes, "The Pricing of Options and Corporate Liabilities", *Journal of Political Economy* 81(3), 1973.

* P. Kloeden and E. Platen, *Numerical Solution of Stochastic Differential Equations* (Springer).

* R. Cont, "Empirical properties of asset returns: stylized facts and statistical issues", *Quantitative Finance* 1(2), 2001.
