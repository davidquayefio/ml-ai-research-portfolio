# ML & AI Research Portfolio

A long-term research portfolio exploring the **mathematical, statistical, and computational foundations of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence**.

The goal of this portfolio is to develop a deeper understanding of **why machine learning methods work**, how they generalize, how optimization affects learning, and how models behave under uncertainty and distribution shift.

My research interests include:

- Mathematical Machine Learning
- Statistical Learning
- Bayesian Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- AI Research
- Financial Machine Learning
- Time-Series & Sequential Learning
- Uncertainty Quantification
- Distribution Shift

## Research Approach

Projects in this portfolio progress from mathematical foundations and classical statistical learning toward deep learning, modern AI, and research-oriented applications.

Each project aims to go beyond implementation by examining:

**Theory → Mathematical Formulation → Experiment → Results → Analysis → Limitations → Future Research**

## Project Areas

### 01. Mathematical Machine Learning
Foundational projects exploring optimization, linear algebra, calculus, and the mathematics behind machine learning algorithms.

### 02. Statistical Learning
Projects focused on probability, statistical inference, Bayesian methods, generalization, and uncertainty.

### 03. Classical Machine Learning
Investigations into traditional ML algorithms, regularization, calibration, model complexity, and generalization.

### 04. Deep Learning
Experiments exploring neural networks, optimization, representation learning, and the mathematical mechanisms behind deep learning.

### 05. Modern AI
Research-oriented projects involving attention, Transformers, transfer learning, uncertainty, and distribution shift.

### 06. Financial Machine Learning
Applications and investigations involving financial prediction, volatility, market regimes, Bayesian methods, and financial uncertainty.

### 07. Time-Series & Sequential Learning
Research into forecasting, sequential models, concept drift, probabilistic forecasting, and temporal machine learning.

### 08. Research Projects
Paper reproductions, extensions, ablation studies, robustness experiments, and eventually independent research questions.

## Long-Term Goal

To develop the mathematical, statistical, computational, and research skills required to contribute to **Machine Learning and AI research**, with particular interests in **Financial ML and time-series learning**.
